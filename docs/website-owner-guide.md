# GoldCode Events website — owner's guide

Standard operating procedures for running goldcodeevents.com.
Version 1 · October 2026 · Prepared by codespecs.net

---

## 1. What you control

The website has one private area, the **manager page**, at:

**goldcodeevents.com/admin**

From there you manage everything about your nights:

| You can change | You cannot change (ask your developer) |
| --- | --- |
| Event title, short name, series | Homepage headlines and brand lines |
| The story paragraphs for each night | The About page |
| Date, start and end time | Footer, contact details, social links |
| Venue, address, city, age note | Colours, fonts, layout |
| Humanitix ticket link and ticket status | Logo |
| Up to ten photographs per night, with captions and order | |
| Whether a night is Published or a Draft | |
| Whether a night is Upcoming or Past | |

Everything you save on the manager page is live on the public site within a few seconds. There is no separate "publish website" step.

---

## 2. Signing in

1. Go to **goldcodeevents.com/admin**.
2. Enter the username and password you were given separately. Never put them in an email, a group chat or this document.
3. Click **Sign in**.

When you are finished, click **Sign out** at the top right, especially on a shared or borrowed computer.

If you forget the password, contact your developer. Do not create extra accounts; the site is built for one manager account.

---

## 3. Reading the manager page

At the top you see three counters: **Published**, **Drafts** and **Photographs**.

Below is the list of nights. Each row shows the night's title, its date and venue line, and three badges:

- **Published** or **Draft** — whether the public can see it.
- **Upcoming** or **Past** — which section of the site it appears in.
- **n/10 photos** — how many photographs it has.

Click a row to open it. Click it again to close it.

## 4. Procedures

### 4.1 Announce a new night

1. Click **New night**.
2. Fill in **Title** (the full name, e.g. *Africa to Aotearoa: The Link Up – Summer Edition*) and **Short name** (how you say it casually, e.g. *The Link Up – Summer Edition*).
3. Optionally paste the first version of the **Story**.
4. Click **Create draft**. The night is created as a **Draft** — the public cannot see it yet.
5. Open the new night in the list and complete the rest of the form (see 4.2).
6. When it is ready, set **Visibility** to **Published** and click **Save night**.

A published upcoming night appears on the Events page. The soonest upcoming night also becomes the headline event on the homepage and controls the **Get tickets** button in the top bar.

### 4.2 Fill in or edit a night

Open the night and work down the form. All fields are optional unless marked.

| Field | What to put in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** (required) | Full event name as it should appear in headings. |
| **Short name** (required) | Shorter name used in lists and photo groups. |
| **Series** | The series it belongs to, e.g. *Africa to Aotearoa*. Leave empty for a one-off. |
| **Place line** | The one-line summary shown under the title on the Events page. Use the pattern `30 May 2026 \| Cassette Nine, Auckland`. The bar `\|` separates the date from the venue. For a night with no fixed date yet, write just the venue or city. |
| **Story** | The paragraphs visitors read. Press Enter twice between paragraphs. Two or three paragraphs is right; the first one is also used as the opening line on the homepage. |
| **Search description** | One plain sentence describing the night. Google shows this under the link. Keep it under about 160 characters. |
| **Starts / Ends** | Date and time in Auckland time. These power the "Doors 9:30 pm–3:00 am" line and the big date on the Events page. |
| **This night has passed** | Tick this after the night has happened (see 4.5). |
| **Venue / City / Address** | Venue name, city, and street address. |
| **Humanitix link** | Paste the full event link from Humanitix (starts with `https://events.humanitix.com/`). Leave empty and the **Get tickets** button is hidden for that night. |
| **Age note** | e.g. *R18 — valid photo ID required*. |
| **Ticket status** | Choose one: Coming soon, Tickets available, Early bird nearly gone, Sold out, Postponed, Cancelled. Shown next to the ticket button. |
| **Visibility** | **Published** = live. **Draft** = hidden from the public. |

Click **Save night**. "Saved." appears under the button when it has worked. If something is wrong, a red message explains what to fix.

After saving a published night, click **View the public page** to check how it looks.

### 4.3 Put tickets on sale

1. Create the event on Humanitix and copy its public link.
2. Open the night, paste the link into **Humanitix link**.
3. Set **Ticket status** to **Tickets available**.
4. Click **Save night**.

The button appears on that night's page, on the Events page, and — if it is the soonest upcoming night — in the top bar of every page.

Sold out? Change **Ticket status** to **Sold out** and save. Leave the link in place so people can still reach the Humanitix page for waitlists.

### 4.4 Postpone or cancel

- Set **Ticket status** to **Postponed** or **Cancelled** and save. The night stays visible with that label.
- When a new date is confirmed, update **Starts**, **Ends** and **Place line**, set status back to **Tickets available**, and save.
- To hide a night completely, set **Visibility** to **Draft**. Nothing is deleted; you can publish it again later.

### 4.5 After the night: mark it past and add photographs

Do this within a week of the event so the site keeps looking current.

1. Open the night.
2. Tick **This night has passed**.
3. Change the first paragraph of the **Story** from future tense to past tense if needed (*"returns"* → *"brought"*).
4. Click **Save night**. The night moves from Upcoming to **Past nights** on the Events page, and the next upcoming night takes over the homepage and the ticket button automatically.
5. Scroll to **Photographs** and upload up to ten (see 4.6).

Once a past night has at least one photograph it appears on the Gallery page and, if it is the most recent past night with photos, in the homepage photo block.

### 4.6 Photographs

**Choosing photos**

- Up to **ten** per night. The eleventh is refused — remove one first.
- Pick photos with lots of people, hands up, the room full. Crowd shots work better than DJ close-ups.
- Use JPG or PNG. Aim for 1–3 MB each; phones and cameras usually export within this range. Very large files (over 10 MB) upload slowly.
- Landscape (wider than tall) suits the layout best. One or two portrait shots are fine.
- Make sure you have the photographer's permission to use the images.

**Adding a photo**

1. Open the night and scroll to **Photographs (n/10)**.
2. Under **Add a photograph**, choose the file.
3. Optionally type a **Caption** (a few words: *"The floor at midnight"*).
4. Click **Upload**. "Photograph added." confirms it.

**Ordering**

The first photograph is the largest on the public page and is the one used on the Events page strip and the homepage. Put your strongest shot first. Use **Move earlier** and **Move later** to change the order.

**Captions**

Edit the caption under any photo and click **Save caption**. Captions are optional and show in the full-screen viewer.

**Removing**

Click **Remove** under the photo. This is immediate and cannot be undone — keep your originals.

## 5. Monthly check (10 minutes)

- Open goldcodeevents.com on your phone. Does the homepage show the right next event? Does **Get tickets** open the right Humanitix page?
- Open the manager page. Any night that has happened but still shows **Upcoming**? Tick **This night has passed** and save.
- Any past night with 0 photos? Add some.
- Any **Draft** that should be live, or a stale draft to tidy?
- Spot-check a past night's page and the Gallery page.

## 6. If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Sign in as the Event Manager." | Your session expired. | Sign out and sign in again. |
| "This account cannot edit events." | You are signed in with an account that is not the manager. | Sign out and use the manager username. |
| "A night needs a title and a name." | Title or Short name is empty. | Fill both and save again. |
| "This night already has ten photographs." | The ten-photo limit. | Remove one, then upload. |
| "Choose a photograph." | You clicked Upload without picking a file. | Pick a file first. |
| Change saved but the public page looks old | Your browser cached the page. | Refresh the page (pull down on mobile, Ctrl+F5 on desktop). |
| Page will not load at all | Hosting or database issue. | Contact your developer. Nothing you did on the manager page can take the site down. |

---

## 7. Good habits

- **Use the manager page only.** Do not ask anyone to "edit the code" for event text; everything about a night is in the form.
- **Draft first, publish when complete.** Visitors should never see a night with no date or venue.
- **One source of truth for dates.** If the date changes, change it here the same day you change it on Humanitix and Instagram.
- **Keep the password private.** Change it if you suspect anyone else has it; ask your developer how.
- **Keep photo originals** on your own drive or cloud. The website is a display, not a backup.
