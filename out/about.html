About GoldCode

Africa in the sound.
Aotearoa in the room.

GoldCode Events is an Auckland-based entertainment company creating premium live experiences through music, culture and unforgettable events.

Why we gather

Music travels.
Culture connects.

We believe a great event can make a city feel smaller and the world feel closer.

Our mission is to unite cultures through music and bring world-class entertainment to Aotearoa. Africa to Aotearoa is our signature series: a home for Afrobeats, Amapiano, Afro Sungura, Dancehall, R&B, Hip Hop and the sounds that sit between them.
